Twenty-four portraits of one genome

Each form is one chromosome, painted from my own raw genotype file. The brush travels once around the circle from one end of the chromosome to the other. Its colour is the ancestry 23andMe assigned to that stretch, one stroke set for each copy. How far it reaches, how thick it grows and where it leaves specks all come from the genotypes underneath: the deep notches are long runs where both copies are identical.

Source: 23andMe raw genotype file (v5 chip, ~630,000 markers) and Ancestry Composition at 50% confidence, processed locally into 2-megabase windows. No individual variants or rsIDs are shown or stored on this page. The mitochondrial genome is left out. A 30× whole-genome layer is restoring from cold storage and can be added later.
