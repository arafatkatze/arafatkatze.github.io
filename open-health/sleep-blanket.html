A blanket knitted from 834 nights of sleep

Every row is one night, read left to right from six in the evening to two the next afternoon. Each stitch is ten minutes, dyed by the sleep stage my watch recorded. Three panels, one per year, from April 2024 to last night. Clock times are as the export stores them, in UTC−8, so nights spent in other time zones drift sideways.

Source: Apple Health export (Apple Watch sleep stages, heart rate, resting heart rate, step count), parsed locally. Nights with under an hour of recorded sleep are left unknit. Times are as stored in the export. Rows at right show hours asleep.
