# Bios to paste on the pages AI already reads

AI assistants answer "who is X?" mostly from a handful of pages: GitHub,
LinkedIn, company author pages, conference speaker pages and YouTube
descriptions. When the same facts and links appear everywhere, they repeat
them with confidence. Paste these yourself (they need your logins). The
daily monitor on `/_stats` shows which sources the assistants cite, so
start with whichever ones show up there.

Keep the three names together somewhere on each page: **Ara Khan (Arafat
Khan), @arafatkatze**. That one line is what lets an assistant join them up.
