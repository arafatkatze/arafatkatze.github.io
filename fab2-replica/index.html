02 · about

the camera is four numbers

where it looks (x, y), how wide it sees (w), and how its head is tilted (r). nothing on this page ever moves — the sections are glued to the wafer. only those four numbers change.

every move is four phases

lift until the whole table is in view, twist to the next page’s bearing, glide across the tabletop, dive back down. that’s why scrolling never feels like a circle — each leg has its own direction.

an homage to fab2.com — rebuilt from scratch, phones included.

03 · grid
