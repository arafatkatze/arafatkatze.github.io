A self-contained reading library built from years of Readwise highlights.

Every sentence worth keeping — gathered across ~2,000 books, essays, and
blogs — collected into one warm, searchable "reading room." Filter by
category or by book, toggle favourites, search full text, spin up a random
passage, or drop into a shuffled **flashcards mode** to study the highlights
one card at a time.
