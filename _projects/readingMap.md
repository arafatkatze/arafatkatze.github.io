A fast, self-contained embedding map of years of reading highlights.

Every highlight is embedded (MiniLM sentence embeddings), projected to 3-D with
UMAP, and clustered into themes (love, money, fear & anxiety, writing, the mind,
relationships, …). The whole layout ships as a tiny pre-gzipped file so it loads
almost instantly, then renders in WebGL as a galaxy you can turn: drag to orbit,
shift-drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, hover (or tap) any star to read the
quote and the book it came from, click a theme to fly to it, or hit the 2D
button to flatten the galaxy into the flat map it grew out of.

A banner links back to [the full, searchable quotes list and flashcards](/reading-quotes/).
