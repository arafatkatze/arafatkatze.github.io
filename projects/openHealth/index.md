# Open-sourcing my health

> Eight years of my own health data, from genome to blood tests to sleep, drawn as three interactive pages, with a short film and the data behind each one.

- Author: Ara Khan (Arafat Khan, @arafatkatze)
- Type: Project
- Category: art
- Canonical page: https://arafatkatze.com/open-health/

Three interactive pages drawn from my own health data: a genome portrait, a
timeline of blood tests and daily activity since 2018, and a blanket knitted
from 834 nights of sleep. The data behind each page is published as JSON.
